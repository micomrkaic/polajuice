What do these cameras and films do?

Cameras

The camera is the format and process: optics, grain, flash, framing. Traits below are generated from the engine's own parameters, so they always match what actually renders.

Films

The film is the color chemistry, applied as a 3D LUT. "Canonical (auto)" picks each camera's period-correct stock; Age is a third axis — storage fog, faded contrast and a magenta drift that works with any camera and film. Developing is the chemistry: push for contrast and grain, pull to soften, or cross-process slide film for shifted colors — disabled for instant cameras, which develop inside the film unit.
